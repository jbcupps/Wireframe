About

About this site

An interactive browser laboratory for curious people exploring non-orientable topology, 4D projections, and spacetime-manifold intuition.

What it is for

Spacetime Manifolds is designed to make unusual geometric ideas easier to meet. Each interactive model pairs a small visual experiment with a question worth carrying forward.

Research and experiments

The topology explorers are learning-oriented entry points. The original research visualizations remain available for deeper experimentation, including the SKB model and the evolution model.
