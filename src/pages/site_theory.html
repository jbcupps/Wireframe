The hypothesis

A topology-first idea about spacetime.

You do not need to accept a theory to explore its questions. This research framework asks whether particle-like behavior could arise from stable structure in spacetime itself.

What stays the same?

Topology studies properties that survive smooth bending and stretching. The models begin by asking which features of a shape are persistent rather than merely visual.

What is a defect?

In this hypothesis, a particle-like feature is a compact, persistent twist or defect in geometry. Orientability, winding, and holonomy help describe what kind of feature it is.

Where does time enter?

The proposal treats spacetime as four-dimensional and Lorentzian. It investigates whether internal closed structure can coexist with constraints that keep the exterior description causal.

How could things interact?

Combinations and transitions are modeled with gluing operations, junctions, and cobordisms rather than only as point-like events. The papers develop the formal version.
